# SAMPLE Attendance Policy

> Demonstration document only. This is fictional sample college information, not an official college policy.

## Minimum attendance

Students should maintain at least **75% attendance** in each course to be eligible for the end-semester examination. A student below this threshold should contact the academic office. Any exception or medical consideration is subject to official college approval.

## Recording attendance

Attendance is recorded for each scheduled class. Students should check the student portal and report a discrepancy to the course instructor within five working days.
