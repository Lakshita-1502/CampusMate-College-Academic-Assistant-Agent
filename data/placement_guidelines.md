# SAMPLE Placement Guidelines

> Fictional demonstration material. Confirm eligibility and document requirements with the placement office.

## Registration documents

Students registering for campus placement should prepare a current resume, college identification card, academic transcripts, and a passport-size photograph. Some employers may request additional documents such as a portfolio or proof of internship.

## Preparation

Attend the placement briefing, review the employer role description, and keep contact details current in the placement portal. Registration does not guarantee an interview or offer.
