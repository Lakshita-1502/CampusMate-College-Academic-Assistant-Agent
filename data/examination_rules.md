# SAMPLE Examination Rules

> Fictional demonstration material. Consult the official examination office for current rules.

## Before the examination

Students should arrive at the examination room at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start and bring their college identification card. Electronic devices must be switched off and stored unless the examination instructions explicitly allow them.

## During the examination

Students must follow invigilator instructions. Sharing materials, communicating with other candidates, or using unauthorized notes is prohibited. Requests to leave the room should be made to an invigilator.
